## Corpus-level cautions

- The interviews contain fewer clean execution-interruption episodes than the
  label “task interruption study” implies.
- P07 is a valuable scheduling-dependency case but should not be analyzed as a
  short interruption/resumption event.
- P08 should not support core findings without a clearly bounded second event.
- P14 should be retained as a countercase rather than forced into the proposed
  mechanism.
- T1–T6 and T7–T14 differ in interviewer and question delivery; protocol batch
  should be retained as an audit attribute.
