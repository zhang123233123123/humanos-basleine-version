# Coded case records v0.1

> **Legacy preliminary artifact:** these case summaries predate the Chinese v0.3 open-coding audit. They must not override the evidence types, identifiers, or boundaries in the five Chinese `开放编码表_*.md` files.

These records paraphrase the transcripts to minimize disclosure. Timestamps
refer to the source transcript and allow an authorized researcher to audit the
interpretation. No quotation in this file should be used in a paper; publication
quotes must be rechecked against the original recording/transcript and
anonymized separately.

## P01-E01

- **Concrete account (`open_prompted`, 00:10:08–00:11:53):** multi-day family
  obligations displaced writing and examination preparation. Work did not begin
  during the planned period and later resumed without an explicit transition.
- **Primary type:** `planned_task_non_start`.
- **Triggers:** `trigger_external`; secondary fatigue after the obligation.
- **Immediate action/outcome:** accepted the displacement; several planned days
  remained inactive; later returned directly to accumulated work.
- **Related state–task account (00:12:46–00:13:34):** delayed problem solving
  until feeling more alert and switched from writing to exercises when emotional
  state made writing difficult.
- **Recovery context (`open_prompted`, 00:18:13–00:18:47):** reread writing and
  preserve references found but not yet read or cited. Codes:
  `checkpoint_progress`, `checkpoint_material_pointer`.
- **System preference (`hypothetical`, 00:14:49–00:20:47):** questioned the
  need for a planning system, warned that persistent completion metrics could
  increase anxiety, and requested objective reasons for task-switch advice.
- **Analytic memo:** strong boundary evidence against constant progress exposure;
  not a clean execution-interruption case.

## P02-E01

- **Primary type:** `planned_task_non_start`.

- **Immediate action/outcome:** rest first and move the day's plan forward.
- **Recovery boundary (`open_prompted`, 00:12:41–00:13:45):** normal breaks were

  the visible previous result cued the next calculation. Codes:
  `recovery_friction:low`, `checkpoint_progress`, `non_need_or_self_sufficiency`.
- **System preference (`hypothetical`, 00:15:22–00:15:41):** preferred direct

- **Analytic memo:** an important countercase: not every pause creates a
  resumption problem, particularly when natural task boundaries externalize
  state.

## P05-E01

  work was pushed forward.
- **Primary type:** `self_initiated_disengagement`.
- **Triggers:** `trigger_task_state` and `trigger_person_state` interacting.
- **Immediate action/outcome:** stop when no further approach was available;
  postpone and later reconstruct the problem.

- **Recovery context (`open_prompted`, 00:15:51):** explored approaches, whether

  automation and did not always require explanations, but suggested choices
  should stay connected to known behavior and current task difficulty.
- **Analytic memo:** strongest case that Reason–Progress–Next Step alone may be
  insufficient for high-context problem solving.
